ABOUT MINIWAVELIST

A leaderboard dedicated to full mini-wave-only Geometry Dash levels.

What is MiniWaveList?

MiniWaveList ranks Geometry Dash levels that are played entirely using the mini-wave gamemode.

Stats Viewer

Each player receives the points assigned to every ranked level they beat. Those points stack, and the Stats Viewer ranks players by their total.

Verifiers and Victors

Each level has one verifier. Its Victors panel lists players confirmed to have beaten that level.
